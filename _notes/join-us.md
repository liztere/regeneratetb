## Ways to Engage

### 1. Attend a Gathering
We'd like to have gatherings more common throughout the year; workshops, convergences, and seasonal celebrations. So stay tuned for announcements!

### 2. Explore the Garden
This knowledge garden is open to all. Wander through the notes, follow the connections.

**New to regenerative thinking?** Start here:
- [[Regenerative Culture]]
- [[Daniel Christian Wahl]]
- [[Living Systems Thinking]]

### 3. Share Your Gifts
Regenerative cultures thrive on gift exchange. What do you have to offer?
- Host a workshop or skillshare
- Share your knowledge or practice
- Offer your land for gatherings
- Help with organizing or communications
- Simply show up and participate


### 4. Connect With Others
We're building a network of regenerative practitioners across the Tampa Bay region.

### 5. Start Where You Are
You don't need to wait for an official gathering. Regeneration begins wherever you are.
